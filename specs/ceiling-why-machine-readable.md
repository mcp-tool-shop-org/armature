# `ceiling.why_machine_readable` - the one home

Every `specs/*seeds.json` carries a `ceiling` block bounding the credits an experiment may
spend. Until swarm wave 28 each of the eight also carried a byte-identical 4,984-character
copy of the paragraph below - 49.9% of every byte under `specs/` - inside that block, so an
operator opening a spec to read a two-line bound scrolled another experiment's citation
history to reach it. `F-7ca576d2` moved the paragraph here and left a one-sentence pointer in
its place. The paragraph itself is unchanged and un-deleted; its RE-ANCHORED notes continue to
be appended in place, now once instead of eight times.

The seven citations below are the call sites of
`build_assembly_payload.read_seed_registration`, the one reader of a seed registration.
`tests/test_seeds_specs.py` resolves every one of them against the tree on every run - a
citation that drifts fails there rather than sitting in a committed spend-ceiling spec as a
placeholder shaped like evidence.

---

The value was free prose and no tool under tools/ read the key, while `seeds` and `allocation` — its machine-readable siblings — are both consumed and gated. CLAUDE.md makes the credit ceiling a per-spec obligation and records that spent credits have no compensator, so the one unrecoverable resource in this repo was bounded by a string nothing could parse. The prose is not deleted: it is `note`, verbatim, and the number a submission step can count against sits beside it. CORRECTION, 2026-09-04 (swarm wave 8, F-6c5c537b). This paragraph used to argue the fix with the clause “while `seeds` and `allocation` — its machine-readable siblings — are both consumed and gated”, and to close “and the number a submission step can count against sits beside it”. Both halves were measured on the tree and only one survived. `grep -rn allocation tools/ --include=*.py` returns 0 hits: `allocation` has no reader at all, so it is exactly as unread as `ceiling` was when it was filed. `seeds` has seven readers — build_animate_payload.py:741 (main), build_camera_i2v_payload.py:1295 (main), build_i2v_payload.py:897 (main), build_lora_arm_payload.py:532 (gate_s), build_r2v_payload.py:535 (build_and_write), build_t2v_payload.py:570 (main) and gate_saved_graph.py:1377 (main) — each handing the list to a seed gate, so that half of the claim holds. And no tool reads `ceiling` from a seeds spec either: every `ceiling` hit under tools/ is `armature_core.assembly`'s unrelated CASCADE slot ceiling, or a builder passing that one. So `ceiling.submissions` is a number a submission step COULD count against and none does today — the bound is held by the spec and the executor, not by code. The original claim is kept above rather than deleted, because the correction is more useful than the original. tests/test_seeds_specs.py pins the measurement; the day a builder counts against `ceiling.submissions`, that test flips with it and this paragraph is rewritten again.
