## The formula family (as the rendition states it)

- **Basic:** `Subject + Scene + Motion`.
- **Advanced:** `Subject(+description) + Scene(+description) + Motion(+description) +
  Camera Language + Atmosphere + Styling`.
- **Camera-led:** for camera-driven shots, the camera-movement description moves to the
  **front** of the prompt, ahead of subject and scene.
- **Transformation:** `Subject A(+state) + transformation process + Subject B(+state) +
  scene / motion / camera / atmosphere / styling`.
- The rendition's own claim (theirs, unmeasured here): the more complete, precise, and
  detailed the prompt, the closer the output tracks intent.

## The dimension vocabulary

- **Shot types:** extreme close-up · close-up · medium shot · long shot · bird's-eye.
- **Angles:** low angle · overhead · first-person / FPV drone.
- **Lenses:** fisheye · wide angle.
- **Camera moves:** dolly in · dolly out · pan · tracking shot · orbit.
- **Speed:** slow motion · fast motion · time-lapse.
- **Atmosphere / style:** single-phrase mood anchors (vibrant, mysterious, lonely,
  tense, majestic) and style anchors (cyberpunk, wasteland, line-art, felt, pixel,
  named-painter styles), used as dedicated prompt slots.

## Techniques the rendition carries

- **Layered prompts:** a primary clause plus secondary layers (background actors
  reacting, the scene evolving, day turning to night).
- **Emotion as visual behavior:** moods written as what is seen (falling leaves, golden
  light), not abstractions.
- **A narrative arc inside one clip:** beginning, middle, end written into the prompt.
